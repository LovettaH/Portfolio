Case 01 — UX Flow Research案例01 — UX流程研究

Case 01 · iOGYM · UX Research · 2024–Now

Why users left. It wasn't the sensors. 為什麼用戶離開。不是感應器的問題。

When everyone assumed users left because sensors were inaccurate, we kept digging. What research actually uncovered: the workout flow itself kept interrupting people. The app would remind you rest time was over, but you still had to tap Start again manually, otherwise nothing would be recorded. No training time, no reps, no eccentric or concentric data. Every set, every session. 當所有人都認為用戶流失是因為感應器不準確時，我們繼續深挖。研究真正揭露的是：訓練流程一直在打斷使用者。App雖然會提醒你休息結束了，但你還是要再手動按一次「開始」，不然當組的訓練時間、次數、向心和離心動作都不會被記錄。每一組，每一次都是如此。

The problem問題所在

What everyone assumed大家原本的假設

Sensor data was inaccurate. Users couldn't trust the equipment readings, so they stopped using the app. Development effort was focused on improving hardware precision. 感應器數據不準確，用戶不信任設備讀數，進而停止使用應用程式。開發資源集中在改善硬體精度上。

What research revealed研究揭示的真相

The app flow was too cumbersome. Users had to open app → scan QR code → select exercise → rest → manually adjust reps and sets. Every single time. 應用程式流程太繁瑣。用戶每次都要：開啟app → 掃描QR碼 → 選擇動作 → 休息 → 手動調整次數和組數。每一次都要重複。

"The main reason users don't continue using the app is friction. Every time you have to scan, every time selecting an exercise takes too long, every time you have to remember your last weight... it's not the inaccurate reps that's driving people away." — research synthesis, April 2024 「使用者沒有持續使用的主要原因是：麻煩。每次都要掃描、每次選動作都很久、每次都要回想自己上次的重量⋯⋯而非表象的次數不準造成使用者沒有繼續使用下去。」 — 研究綜合結論，2024年4月

Key insights關鍵洞察

01

Friction in the workout flow was the #1 churn driver. Not sensor accuracy.訓練流程中的摩擦感才是流失的主因，而非感應器精度。

02

Users don't look at their phones mid-workout. The app needs to work without constant interaction.使用者在訓練中不會看螢幕。App需要在無需持續互動的情況下運作。

03

Users wanted the app to remember their previous weights and exercises. Starting from scratch every time was the dealbreaker.用戶希望app能記住上次的重量與動作。每次從頭開始是最大的使用障礙。

Reflection反思

The biggest lesson was separating the assumed problem from the real one. Stakeholders were convinced the sensors were the issue. Going directly to users, through surveys, coach interviews, and gym usability visits, revealed that friction was in the journey, not the data. And once the finding was clear, the harder work was staying consistent about it: keeping the research visible, making sure it stayed part of how decisions got made. 最大的收穫是學會區分假設的問題和真實的問題。利害關係人深信感應器是問題所在，但透過問卷、教練訪談和健身房易用性觀察，發現摩擦感其實來自使用流程，而非數據本身。找到答案之後，更難的事情是讓它真正被看見：持續讓研究結果留在討論桌上，確保它成為產品決策的一部分。

Reflection反思

The original product tracked individual exercise data, but the real customer is the gym, not the member. The new direction: help gyms predict churn and intervene at the right moment, for the right member, in the right way. Like Netflix recommending what to watch next, or Spotify's Discover Weekly, anticipating needs before they're expressed. The hardest insight was restraint: knowing when NOT to intervene matters just as much as knowing when to act. 這項研究從根本上重新定義了產品策略。原本的產品追蹤個人運動數據，但真正的客戶是健身房，不是會員。新方向：幫助健身房預測流失風險，在正確的時機、對正確的會員、以正確的方式介入。就像Netflix推薦你接下來想看什麼，或是Spotify的發現週報，在需求被表達之前就預測到它。最難的洞察是克制：知道何時不介入，和知道何時介入同樣重要。

Case 02 — Visual Design案例02 — 視覺設計

Case 02 · Visual Design · iOGYM · 2024–Now案例02 · 視覺設計 · iOGYM · 2024至今

Making complex fitness data feel simple. 讓複雜的健身數據變得易懂。

Designing weekly and monthly training reports for a fitness app with a context-aware interface: neon during active training sets, dark during rest. Translating multi-dimensional performance data into clean, motivating visuals. 為一款配合訓練情境切換介面的健身應用程式設計週報與月報，將多維度的表現數據轉化為清晰、激勵人心的視覺呈現。

Role職責UX · Visual DesignUX · 視覺設計
ToolsFigma

Design principles設計原則

01

Non-judgmental: data shows progress without making users feel bad about gaps or missed sessions不批判性：數據展示進步，而不讓用戶對缺席感到難受

Data Overview數據總覽

Volume by weekday, not just weekly total按訓練日查看，而不只是週總量

Users who split upper and lower body training see very different volume on different days. A single weekly total masks the real pattern.分上下肢訓練的使用者，每天的訓練量差異很大。單一的週總量數字具有誤導性，掩蓋了真實的訓練模式。

Design decision: Weekday tabs let users drill into each training day individually, making comparison meaningful rather than averaged out.設計決策：增加星期分頁，讓使用者能單獨查看每個訓練日，讓訓練量比較有意義而非被平均掉。

Training Time Pattern訓練時段分析

Your most efficient time. No lecture, just insight.你最有效率的訓練時段。只給洞察，不說教。

People train at wildly different times, and all of them are valid. The heatmap shows when each person actually shows up most consistently, so they can recognise their own natural rhythm. Not to correct it, just to see it.每個人的訓練時間都不一樣，而且都是有效的。熱力圖顯示每個人實際最一致出現的時段，讓他們能認識自己的自然節奏。不是為了糾正，只是讓他們看見。

Design decision: No "optimal training time" label. No suggestion to change habits. Just a gentle mirror: you show up at this time. Showing up at all is what matters most.設計決策：沒有「最佳訓練時間」的標籤。不建議改變習慣。只是一面溫柔的鏡子：你在這個時段出現。能夠出現，本身就是最重要的事。

Muscle Distribution肌肉分佈

See your training balance in colour.用顏色一眼看出訓練平衡。

The body heatmap uses three colours: 極低 (light), 中強 (medium), 極強 (intense), so users can instantly see which muscle groups they're hammering and which they're neglecting. No numbers required to understand the pattern.身體熱力圖使用三種顏色：極低（淺色）、中強（中色）、極強（深色），讓使用者立刻看出哪些肌群在高強度訓練，哪些被忽略了。不需要看數字就能理解模式。

Design decision: Colour over numbers at a glance. The body silhouette maps intensity to anatomy; users read their own body, not a spreadsheet.設計決策：一眼看顏色，而非數字。身體輪廓圖將強度映射到解剖位置，使用者讀的是自己的身體，不是試算表。

Training Quality & Tempo訓練品質與節奏

Connect tempo to outcome.讓訓練節奏和目標產生連結。

Different tempos produce different results: hypertrophy, strength, explosiveness. Most users don't know what tempo they're training at, or what it means for their goals.不同的訓練節奏會產生不同的效果，包括增肌、力量和爆發力。大多數使用者不知道自己實際的訓練節奏，也不知道這對目標意味著什麼。

Design decision: Show the 增肌/力量/爆發 tempo distribution so users can compare their actual training style against what they want to achieve.設計決策：顯示增肌、力量、爆發節奏的分佈，讓使用者可以對比自己的實際訓練風格和訓練目標。

Monthly report每月報告

Monthly Achievements月度成就

Specific PRs. Exact dates. Real proof.具體紀錄。確切日期。真實的證明。

Generic "great job!" doesn't feel earned. But seeing "臥推 8→10下 +20% on 11.25." That's yours. That's specific. That happened.泛泛的「做得好！」不會讓人感到有成就感。但看到「臥推 8→10下 +20%，11月25日」，那是屬於你的。那是具體的。那真的發生了。

Design decision: Every PR shows the exact date and percentage gain. No rounding, no generalising. The user earned this. They deserve the full detail.設計決策：每個個人紀錄都顯示確切日期和百分比增幅。不四捨五入，不概括。使用者努力贏得了這些，他們值得看到完整的細節。

Monthly Score月度總分

Four dimensions. One score. All encouraging.四個維度。一個分數。全都是鼓勵。

The score is made of four dimensions, each designed to reward sustainable habits rather than peak performance:分數由四個維度組成，每個維度都被設計來獎勵可持續的習慣，而非巔峰表現：

持續性 (max 40): Training days ÷ target × 40. Bonus +2 for 5 consecutive days. Penalty -2 for skipping 5 in a row. Consistency is the only metric that really matters.持續性（最高40分）：訓練日 ÷ 目標訓練日 × 40。連續練5天 +2分。連續5天沒練 -2分。持續性才是真正重要的指標。

訓練量: Maintaining volume is already a win. No need to always push more.訓練量：維持住訓練量就已經很好了。不需要每次都更多。

平衡: Push and pull both matter. Balance protects the body long-term.平衡：推和拉都同樣重要。平衡能長期保護身體。

恢復狀態: Staying within a healthy RPE range is the goal. Not maxing out every session.恢復狀態：保持在健康的RPE範圍內才是目標。不是每次都要榨乾自己。

Volume Trend訓練量趨勢

The average line tells the story at a glance.平均線讓人一眼看出高低。

Weekly numbers alone are hard to judge. Is this week good or bad? The key design element: a large average number on the left, plus a horizontal reference line across the chart so users instantly see which weeks were above or below their own average. No mental maths required.單看週數字很難判斷。這週是好是壞？核心設計元素：左側顯示大的平均數字，圖表上有一條水平參考線，讓使用者立刻看出哪幾週高於或低於自己的平均，不需要心算。

Design decision: The highlighted average line is the anchor. Users compare themselves to themselves, not to other people or arbitrary standards.設計決策：高亮的平均線是基準。使用者與自己比較，不是與他人或任意標準比較。

Muscle Group Breakdown肌群訓練分析

Which muscles are you actually training most?你實際上最常訓練哪些肌群？

Users often don't realise how skewed their training is across muscle groups. This screen shows two things at once: the percentage of sets per muscle group, and the total volume (kg) per week as bars, with a white average line so any week that was heavier or lighter than usual stands out immediately.使用者通常不知道自己的訓練在肌群之間有多不均衡。這個畫面同時顯示兩件事：各肌群的組數比例，以及每週的總重量（kg）柱狀圖，加上白色平均線，讓任何高於或低於平均的週份立刻顯現。

Design decision: Set percentage (left) + weekly volume bars + white average line. Three layers of information in one view, without overwhelming the user.設計決策：組數比例（左側）+ 每週重量柱狀圖 + 白色平均線。一個畫面三層資訊，但不會讓使用者感到不知所措。

Body vs Training身體與訓練的關係

Does training actually change the body? See it directly.訓練真的改變了身體嗎？直接看見。

This is the most complex screen: a dual y-axis chart showing body metrics (muscle mass, weight, body fat %) and training metrics together, both expressed as percentage change (−20% to +20%). When body and training indicators move together, the curves shift noticeably. When nothing much changes, the lines stay flat.這是最複雜的畫面，以雙Y軸圖表，同時顯示身體指標（肌肉量、體重、體脂率）和訓練指標，兩者都以百分比變化（-20%到+20%）呈現。當身體和訓練指標同步變動時，曲線會有明顯的起伏。當變化不大時，曲線就會平緩。

Design decision: Percentage change (not absolute numbers) makes muscle gain and volume comparable on the same axis. A flat curve isn't failure; it means the body is stable. A dramatic curve means something changed.設計決策：使用百分比變化（而非絕對數字），讓肌肉增長和訓練量可以在同一個軸上比較。平緩的曲線不是失敗，代表身體狀態穩定。明顯的曲線代表有什麼改變了。

Reflection反思

The tricky part wasn't making the screens look good. It was about being honest about what the data could and couldn't actually tell you. Our sensor data isn't perfect. Some metrics take weeks to show any meaningful change. Some users' InBody numbers don't match how they actually feel. I didn't want the reports to make false promises, but I also didn't want them to feel flat or pointless. So every design decision kept coming back to the same question: is this showing something real, or am I just making the numbers look better than they are? 難的不是把畫面做得好看，而是要對數據能說什麼、不能說什麼保持誠實。我們的感應器數據並不完美，有些指標要好幾週才會有明顯變化，有些使用者的InBody數字跟他們實際感受到的進步完全對不上。我不想讓報表做出假承諾，但也不想讓它看起來毫無意義。所以每一個設計決定都一直繞回同一個問題：這樣呈現是在傳達真實的事情，還是只是把數字包裝得比實際更漂亮？

Weightlifting habits research · 2025 · N=30 重訓習慣研究 · 2025 · N=30

Weightlifters don't quit.
They evolve. 重訓者，不是不練了。
是進化了。

We surveyed 30 weightlifters across Taiwan and found that "stopping weight training" is almost a myth. They don't give up; they let exercise become part of a bigger life. This finding changed how we think about gym retention. 我們調查了30位來自台灣各地的重訓者，發現「停止重訓」幾乎是個假命題。他們不是放棄，而是讓運動成為了更大生活的一部分。這個發現改變了我們對健身房留客問題的理解方式。

Finding 01核心發現 01

Most people keep moving. The form just changes. 絕大多數人持續在動，只是方式變了

Stopping weight training almost never happens. Respondents either continue lifting, or expand into other activities like yoga, dance, snowboarding, triathlons. Weight training is the starting point, not the destination. 「停止重訓」幾乎不存在。受訪者要嘛繼續重訓，要嘛在重訓的基礎上擴展到其他運動。重訓是起點，不是終點。

Finding 02核心發現 02

Physical change unlocks a bigger life 身體的改變，開啟更積極的生活

Finding 03核心發現 03

The obstacles are external, not internal 阻力通常是外部的，不是內心放棄

When people stop, it's almost always external, like work, injury, or stress. Not a loss of motivation. Most "churned" members still want to come back. 當人們停止重訓，幾乎都是外部原因，像工作、受傷、壓力。不是失去動力。大多數「流失」的會員其實還有回來的意願。

What this means for gyms對健身房的意義

How these findings help improve retention 這些發現，如何幫助健身房留住會員

Most "lost" members haven't given up; they've been interrupted. The strategy should shift from winning them back to helping them not break the habit in the first place. 大多數「流失」的會員不是真的放棄，而是被外部因素打斷了。策略應該從「吸引回來」轉向「幫助他們不要中斷」。

01

Redefine the churn signal重新定義流失的訊號

Two weeks without a visit is not necessarily churn; it might just be a busy work period. Gyms need early-warning systems that identify high-risk windows and reach out before members disappear, not after. 兩週沒來不一定是要放棄，可能只是工作很忙。健身房需要識別每個會員的高風險期，提前做溫暖接觸，而不是等消失了才發優惠券。

02

Use capability language, not appearance language用能力感而非體態來溝通

"You deadlifted 80kg last time. Come back and beat it." lands better than "Summer is coming, get in shape!" The research shows capability, not appearance, is the real driver. 「你上次深蹲了80公斤，這週來繼續突破？」比「夏天快到了，練起來！」更能觸動真實動機。研究顯示能力感才是真正的驅動力。

03

Don't push social on lone wolves不要對獨行俠推銷社交

Some members explicitly prefer solo training, in and out, focused, efficient. Pushing buddy programs on them doesn't just fail, it annoys them. Member type determines the right intervention. 部分重訓者明確表示喜歡獨自訓練。對這類會員推銷找訓練夥伴，不但無效，還可能讓他們感到被打擾。不同類型的會員需要不同的介入方式。

04

Position the gym as the start of a bigger journey把重訓定位為更大生活的入口

Members who lift weights go on to try more: snowboarding, triathlons, dance. Gyms that position themselves as the foundation of an active life, not just a room with equipment, build deeper loyalty. 很多人從重訓出發，進而嘗試更多挑戰。把健身房定位為積極生活的起點，而不只是一個有器材的空間，能建立更深的會員忠誠度。

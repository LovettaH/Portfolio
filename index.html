Case Study 01 · iOGYM · 2024–Now

Why users left. It wasn't the sensors. 為什麼用戶離開。不是感應器的問題。

When everyone assumed users left because sensors were inaccurate, user research revealed a completely different culprit: the app was just too troublesome to use mid-workout. 當所有人都認為用戶流失是因為感應器不準確時，使用者研究揭示了完全不同的原因：在訓練中使用這款應用程式太麻煩了。

Role職責UX Research · PM使用者研究 · 產品管理
Methods方法User interviews · Flow analysis用戶訪談 · 流程分析
Team團隊9 people, cross-functional9人跨職能團隊
Outcome成果Redirected dev priorities重新調整開發優先順序

The problem問題所在

What everyone assumed大家原本的假設

Sensor data was inaccurate. Users couldn't trust the equipment readings, so they stopped using the app. Development effort was focused on improving hardware precision. 感應器數據不準確，用戶不信任設備讀數，進而停止使用應用程式。開發資源集中在改善硬體精度上。

What research revealed研究揭示的真相

The app flow was too cumbersome. Users had to open app → scan QR code → select exercise → rest → manually adjust reps and sets. Every single time. 應用程式流程太繁瑣。用戶每次都要：開啟app → 掃描QR碼 → 選擇動作 → 休息 → 手動調整次數和組數。每一次都要重複。

"The main reason users don't continue using the app is friction. Every time you have to scan, every time selecting an exercise takes too long, every time you have to remember your last weight... it's not the inaccurate reps that's driving people away." — research synthesis, April 2024 「使用者沒有持續使用的主要原因是：麻煩。每次都要掃描、每次選動作都很久、每次都要回想自己上次的重量⋯⋯而非表象的次數不準造成使用者沒有繼續使用下去。」 — 研究綜合結論，2024年4月

The questionnaire ran April 1–24, collecting 10+ new records that month. Interviews were conducted with coaches (Anita, Jackie, Oscar) and competitive athletes. Key observation: most users never looked at the screen mid-workout — they pocketed their phone and only checked it during rest breaks. 問卷調查從4月1日至24日進行，當月新增10筆以上紀錄。訪談對象包含教練（Anita、Jackie、Oscar）和競賽選手。關鍵觀察：大部分使用者在運動時都不會看螢幕——他們把手機放進口袋，只在組間休息時才拿出來看。

Survey — friction findings問卷——操作摩擦力

"Too many exercises to choose from" and "no tutorial." StepX coaches said the repeated manual adjustments made them not want to keep using it. 「選擇動作太多不好挑選」和「沒有教學」。StepX教練大多反應因為麻煩（需要反覆調整）而不想持續使用。

Key insights關鍵洞察

01

Friction in the workout flow was the #1 churn driver. Not sensor accuracy.訓練流程中的摩擦感才是流失的主因，而非感應器精度。

02

Users don't look at their phones mid-workout. The app needs to work without constant interaction.使用者在訓練中不會看螢幕。App需要在無需持續互動的情況下運作。

03

Users wanted the app to remember their previous weights and exercises. Starting from scratch every time was the dealbreaker.用戶希望app能記住上次的重量與動作。每次從頭開始是最大的使用障礙。

Finding validated internally內部驗證

After sharing the synthesis with the team, Tony (a newly hired colleague) confirmed the same finding independently — users consistently cited friction and cumbersome flow, not inaccuracy, as their reason for stopping. 將研究綜合結論分享給團隊後，新同事Tony也獨立驗證了相同的發現——用戶一致表示因為流程麻煩而停止使用，而非數據不準確。

Design decisions設計決策

Immediate priorities立即執行項目

Auto-remember last training weight. Simplify QR code flow. Allow editing past records. Background operation so screen doesn't need to stay on. 記錄上次訓練重量。簡化QR碼流程。可編輯修改訓練紀錄。App可背景作業，螢幕不需要持續開著。

Future vision: NFC flow未來願景：NFC流程

Like pressing play on YouTube — place phone on NFC rack, workout starts automatically. Zero manual steps to begin training. 像YouTube一樣，放上手機架就可以立刻運動，什麼都不用選。零手動步驟開始訓練。

Reflection反思

The biggest lesson was separating the assumed problem from the real one. Stakeholders were convinced the sensors were the issue. Going directly to users — through surveys, coach interviews, and gym usability visits — revealed that friction was in the journey, not the data. This research also taught me how to build a research habit and advocate for findings within a donor-driven product environment where user research wasn't formally part of the role. 最大的收穫是學會區分假設的問題和真實的問題。利害關係人深信感應器是問題所在。但透過問卷、教練訪談和健身房易用性觀察，發現摩擦感來自使用流程，而非數據本身。這次研究也讓我學會如何在捐助者主導的產品環境中建立研究習慣，並在沒有正式研究職責的情況下為用戶需求發聲。

Case Study 02 · iOGYM B2B Research · 2025

What do gyms actually need from their data? 健身房真正需要的資料洞察是什麼？

iOGYM sells to gyms, not individual users. The real question is not "why do users churn" but "what data can help gyms prevent churn before it happens?" iOGYM的客戶是健身房，不是個別使用者。真正的問題不是「為什麼用戶流失」，而是「什麼數據能幫助健身房在流失發生前預防它？」

Role職責Product Research · Strategy產品研究 · 策略
Methods方法Questionnaire · Interviews問卷 · 訪談
Responses問卷回覆30
Status狀態In progress進行中

The real customer problem真正的客戶問題

iOGYM's real customer is the gym, not the individual member. But our product was designed around individual tracking. This misalignment meant gyms couldn't see the value. We weren't solving their actual problem: member retention. iOGYM真正的客戶是健身房，不是個別會員。但我們的產品是圍繞個人追蹤設計的。這個錯位意味著健身房看不到價值，因為我們沒有解決他們真正的問題：會員留存率。

Who pays us誰付費給我們

The gym: our B2B customer健身房，也就是我們的B2B客戶

Who we were designing for我們在為誰設計

The individual gym member, the end user個別健身房會員，也就是終端使用者

Research findings研究發現

01

Physical results unlock a bigger life. Members stay when they feel capable and see progress.身體上的成果開啟更積極的生活。當會員感受到能力提升並看見進步，他們就會留下來。

02

Most members continue exercising after stopping weight training. They transition, not quit.大多數會員停止重訓後仍繼續運動。他們是轉換，不是放棄。

03

The "lone wolf" lifter trains solo by choice. Pushing social connection would feel wrong.「獨行俠」型重訓者選擇獨自訓練。推銷社交連結只會適得其反。

Proposed product direction產品方向提案

Early warning dashboard預警儀表板

Show gyms which members are at risk of churning, and suggest what type of intervention fits each one.向健身房展示哪些會員有流失風險，並建議每位會員適合的介入方式。

Each gym has different classes and community structures. iOGYM should provide default tags, but gyms can create their own on top.每家健身房有不同的課程和社群結構。iOGYM提供預設標籤，但健身房可以在此基礎上自訂自己的標籤。

Reflection反思

This research reframed the entire product strategy: from tracking exercise data to helping gyms understand and retain their members. The most important insight was knowing when NOT to intervene. Pushing social connection on a lone wolf lifter creates a worse experience than no intervention at all. 這項研究重新定義了整體產品策略，從追蹤運動數據出發，轉向幫助健身房了解並留住會員。最重要的洞察是知道何時不應該介入。對喜歡獨自訓練的重訓者推銷社交連結，反而造成比不介入更差的體驗。

Weightlifting habits research · 2025 · N=30 重訓習慣研究 · 2025 · N=30

Weightlifters don't quit.
They evolve. 重訓者，不是不練了。
是進化了。

We surveyed 30 weightlifters across Taiwan and found that "stopping weight training" is almost a myth. They don't give up — they let exercise become part of a bigger life. This finding changed how we think about gym retention. 我們調查了30位來自台灣各地的重訓者，發現「停止重訓」幾乎是個假命題。他們不是放棄，而是讓運動成為了更大生活的一部分。這個發現改變了我們對健身房留客問題的理解方式。

Finding 01核心發現 01

Most people keep moving. The form just changes. 絕大多數人持續在動，只是方式變了

Stopping weight training almost never happens. Respondents either continue lifting, or expand into other activities — yoga, dance, snowboarding, triathlons. Weight training is the starting point, not the destination. 「停止重訓」幾乎不存在。受訪者要嘛繼續重訓，要嘛在重訓的基礎上擴展到其他運動。重訓是起點，不是終點。

Physical change unlocks a bigger life 身體的改變，開啟更積極的生活

The strongest motivation to keep training is not appearance. It's capability — feeling able to do more in life. This sense of physical empowerment is the real retention driver. 持續重訓最強的動力不是外表，而是能力感——感覺能在生活中做更多事。這種身體賦能感才是真正的留存動力。

When people stop, it's almost always external — work, injury, stress. Not a loss of motivation. Most "churned" members still want to come back. 當人們停止重訓，幾乎都是外部原因——工作、受傷、壓力。不是失去動力。大多數「流失」的會員其實還有回來的意願。

What this means for gyms對健身房的意義

How these findings help improve retention 這些發現，如何幫助健身房留住會員

Most "lost" members haven't given up — they've been interrupted. The strategy should shift from winning them back to helping them not break the habit in the first place. 大多數「流失」的會員不是真的放棄，而是被外部因素打斷了。策略應該從「吸引回來」轉向「幫助他們不要中斷」。

01

Redefine the churn signal重新定義流失的訊號

Two weeks without a visit is not necessarily churn — it might just be a busy work period. Gyms need early-warning systems that identify high-risk windows and reach out before members disappear, not after. 兩週沒來不一定是要放棄——可能只是工作很忙。健身房需要識別每個會員的高風險期，提前做溫暖接觸，而不是等消失了才發優惠券。

02

Use capability language, not appearance language用能力感而非體態來溝通

"You deadlifted 80kg last time. Come back and beat it." lands better than "Summer is coming, get in shape!" The research shows capability, not appearance, is the real driver. 「你上次深蹲了80公斤，這週來繼續突破？」比「夏天快到了，練起來！」更能觸動真實動機。研究顯示能力感才是真正的驅動力。

03

Don't push social on lone wolves不要對獨行俠推銷社交

Some members explicitly prefer solo training — in and out, focused, efficient. Pushing buddy programs on them doesn't just fail, it annoys them. Member type determines the right intervention. 部分重訓者明確表示喜歡獨自訓練。對這類會員推銷找訓練夥伴，不但無效，還可能讓他們感到被打擾。不同類型的會員需要不同的介入方式。

04

Position the gym as the start of a bigger journey把重訓定位為更大生活的入口

Members who lift weights go on to try more — snowboarding, triathlons, dance. Gyms that position themselves as the foundation of an active life, not just a room with equipment, build deeper loyalty. 很多人從重訓出發，進而嘗試更多挑戰。把健身房定位為積極生活的起點，而不只是一個有器材的空間，能建立更深的會員忠誠度。

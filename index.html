Case Study 01 · iOGYM · 2024–Now

Why users left — it wasn't the sensors. 為什麼用戶離開——不是感應器的問題。

When everyone assumed users left because sensors were inaccurate, user research revealed a completely different culprit: the app was just too troublesome to use mid-workout. 當所有人都認為用戶流失是因為感應器不準確時，使用者研究揭示了完全不同的原因：在訓練中使用這款應用程式太麻煩了。

Role職責UX Research · PM使用者研究 · 產品管理
Methods方法User interviews · Flow analysis用戶訪談 · 流程分析
Team團隊9 people, cross-functional9人跨職能團隊
Outcome成果Redirected dev priorities重新調整開發優先順序

The problem問題所在

What everyone assumed大家原本的假設

Sensor data was inaccurate — users couldn't trust the equipment readings, so they stopped using the app. Development effort was focused on improving hardware precision. 感應器數據不準確——用戶不信任設備讀數，所以停止使用應用程式。開發資源集中在改善硬體精度上。

What research revealed研究揭示的真相

The app flow was too cumbersome. Users had to open app → scan QR code → select exercise → rest → manually adjust reps and sets. Every single time. 應用程式流程太繁瑣。用戶每次都要：開啟app → 掃描QR碼 → 選擇動作 → 休息 → 手動調整次數和組數。每一次都要重複。

"I have to open the app, scan, select the exercise, go to rest and then adjust my figures — reps and sets... it's just too much." — gym user interview 「我必須開啟app、掃描、選擇動作、去休息，然後再回來調整次數和組數……真的太麻煩了。」 — 健身房用戶訪談

Key insights關鍵洞察

01

Friction in the workout flow was the #1 churn driver — not sensor accuracy訓練流程中的摩擦感才是流失的主因——不是感應器精度

02

Users wanted the app to remember them — previous weights, last reps, last exercise用戶希望app能記住他們——上次的重量、次數、動作

03

Encouragement and progress feedback increased motivation to return鼓勵訊息和進度回饋能提升用戶回訪動機

Design decisions設計決策

Immediate fixes立即改善

Auto-remember previous weight inputs. Add encouragement messages post-workout — "you lifted 5kg more than last time." Weekly and monthly progress reports. 自動記住上次輸入的重量。訓練後加入鼓勵訊息——「你這次比上次多舉了5公斤」。每週和每月進度報告。

Future vision — NFC flow未來願景——NFC流程

Phone rack with NFC: user places phone on rack → workout starts automatically → adjust reps during rest. Zero friction entry point. NFC手機架：用戶將手機放上架子 → app自動開啟並開始記錄 → 休息時調整次數。零摩擦的訓練入口。

Current flow現有流程

Open app → Scan QR → Select exercise → Rest → Return → Adjust reps/sets開啟app → 掃描QR碼 → 選擇動作 → 休息 → 返回 → 調整次數/組數

NFC flowNFC流程

Place phone on rack → App auto-opens → Workout starts → Adjust during rest放上手機 → App自動開啟 → 訓練開始 → 休息時調整

Reflection反思

The biggest lesson was learning to separate the assumed problem from the real one. Stakeholders were convinced the sensors were the issue — but going directly to users revealed the friction was in the journey, not the data. Working within a donor-driven product environment also taught me how to advocate for user needs within real organisational constraints. 最大的收穫是學會區分假設的問題和真實的問題。利害關係人深信感應器是問題所在——但直接訪談用戶後，發現摩擦感來自於使用流程，而非數據本身。在捐助者主導的產品環境中工作，也讓我學會如何在真實的組織限制中為用戶需求發聲。

Case Study 02 · iOGYM B2B Research · 2025

What do gyms actually need from their data? 健身房真正需要的資料洞察是什麼？

iOGYM sells to gyms, not individual users. So the real question isn't "why do users churn" — it's "what data can help gyms prevent churn before it happens?" iOGYM的客戶是健身房，不是個別使用者。所以真正的問題不是「為什麼用戶流失」——而是「什麼數據能幫助健身房在流失發生前預防它？」

Role職責Product Research · Strategy產品研究 · 策略
Methods方法Questionnaire · Interviews問卷 · 訪談
Responses問卷回覆23
Status狀態In progress進行中

The real customer problem真正的客戶問題

iOGYM's real customer is the gym, not the individual member. But our product was designed around individual tracking. This misalignment meant gyms couldn't see the value — because we weren't solving their actual problem: member retention. iOGYM真正的客戶是健身房，不是個別會員。但我們的產品是圍繞個人追蹤設計的。這個錯位意味著健身房看不到價值——因為我們沒有解決他們真正的問題：會員留存率。

Who pays us誰付費給我們

The gym — our B2B customer健身房——我們的B2B客戶

Who we were designing for我們在為誰設計

The individual gym member — the end user個別健身房會員——終端使用者

Research findings研究發現

01

Physical results unlock a bigger life — members stay when they feel capable and see progress身體上的成果開啟更積極的生活——當會員感受到能力提升並看見進步時，他們就會留下來

02

Most members continue exercising after stopping weight training — they transition, not quit大多數會員停止重訓後仍繼續運動——他們是轉換，不是放棄

03

The "lone wolf" lifter trains solo by choice — pushing social connection would feel wrong「獨行俠」型重訓者選擇獨自訓練——推銷社交連結會適得其反

The tag system標籤系統

Member tags need to combine multiple data sources. A member who trains alone + never joins classes + attends consistently = true lone wolf. Don't intervene socially. 會員標籤需要結合多種資料來源。獨自訓練 + 從不參加課程 + 出席穩定 = 真正的獨行俠。不要進行社交介入。

Sensor data (iOGYM)感應器資料（iOGYM）

Training frequency · Equipment used · Session duration · Weight progression · Exercise variety訓練頻率 · 使用器材 · 訓練時長 · 重量進展 · 動作多樣性

Gym data (needs partnership)健身房資料（需合作取得）

Check-in records · Class registration · Membership plan · Days since last visit · Event participation報到記錄 · 課程報名 · 會員方案 · 距上次到訪天數 · 活動參與

[ Insert: tag system diagram / dashboard mockup ][ 插入：標籤系統圖 / 儀表板原型 ]

Proposed product direction產品方向提案

Early warning dashboard預警儀表板

Show gyms which members are at risk of churning — and what type of intervention each one needs before they disappear.向健身房展示哪些會員有流失風險——以及在他們消失前，每位會員需要什麼樣的介入方式。

Configurable platform可客製化平台

Each gym has different classes and community structures. iOGYM should provide default tags, but gyms can create their own on top.每家健身房有不同的課程和社群結構。iOGYM提供預設標籤，但健身房可以在此基礎上自訂自己的標籤。

Reflection反思

This research reframed the entire product strategy — from "track individual exercise data accurately" to "help gyms understand and retain their members." The most important insight was knowing when NOT to intervene: pushing social connection on a lone wolf lifter would create a worse experience than no intervention at all. 這項研究重新定義了整體產品策略——從「準確追蹤個人運動數據」到「幫助健身房了解並留住會員」。最重要的洞察是知道何時不應該介入：對喜歡獨自訓練的重訓者推銷社交連結，反而會造成比不介入更差的體驗。

Making complex fitness data feel simple. 讓複雜的健身數據變得易懂。

Designing weekly and monthly training reports for a dark-mode fitness app — translating multi-dimensional performance data into clean, motivating visuals. 為深色模式的健身應用程式設計每週和每月訓練報告——將多維度的表現數據轉化為清晰、激勵人心的視覺呈現。

Role職責UX · Visual DesignUX · 視覺設計
ToolsFigma
Platform平台iOS dark modeiOS 深色模式

Design principles設計原則

01

Non-judgmental — data shows progress without making users feel bad about gaps or missed sessions不批判性——數據展示進步，而不讓用戶對間隔或缺席感到難受

02

Dark mode first — gym environment, low light, glare reduction on phone screens深色模式優先——健身房環境、低光照、減少手機螢幕眩光

03

Familiar patterns — iPhone Health-inspired layouts reduce cognitive load for new users熟悉的設計模式——參考iPhone健康應用的版面，降低新用戶的認知負擔

Reflection反思

The challenge was designing for data that was sometimes unreliable. Good data visualization isn't just about aesthetics: it's about honest communication, especially when the underlying data has limitations. The design had to feel motivating without overclaiming what the data could prove. 挑戰在於為有時不可靠的數據進行設計。好的數據視覺化不只是美觀問題：它是關於誠實的溝通，尤其是當底層數據有限制時。設計必須讓人感到激勵，同時不過度宣稱數據能證明的事情。
